# CazCad development update for November 17, 2026

**Linux.** CazCad opened a four-solid assembly in Ubuntu 24.04.1 under WSL2 with Mesa software rendering. The assembly was saved as a native CazCad design, reopened, exported to STEP and read back. All four solids were present and the measured volume changed by less than 0.000001 mm³. The Linux launcher also passed all 19 startup checks.

**Windows.** A fix for an Extrude Join preview crash on textured parts and with Ambient Shadows has passed 115 focused preview and appearance tests. The wider Windows regression suite is in progress.

**Tools.** The current development baseline has implementations for 296 of 439 registered commands. Availability depends on the active workspace, selection and editing mode. Rule Fillet, Pattern on Path, Rib, Web and Boundary Fill are in integration testing.
